Can I get clothes altered the same day in Dubai?

Some alteration services offer same day completion, usually for simpler jobs or garments dropped off early in the day. Check with your chosen branch, as timing depends on the garment and the complexity of the work.

Do tailors in Dubai offer home alteration services?

Yes, several tailors in this guide offer a home service or home visits, and one uses a van fitted with sewing machines. Availability can vary by area, so confirm with the business that it covers your location.

What should I bring to an alteration fitting?

Bring the garment itself, along with the shoes and undergarments you plan to wear with it, so the tailor can judge the fit accurately. It also helps to explain clearly what you want changed before the tailor starts pinning.

How do I choose the right alteration service?

Start with location and convenience, then look at whether the service handles your type of garment, such as suits, bridal gowns or abayas. Reading the details on each official website and asking about turnaround times will help you decide.
