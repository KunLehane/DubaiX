PRO VISA UAE

A good fit for: Family and freelance visas in Deira

PRO VISA UAE provides visa and business licensing support for individuals, families and companies in Dubai. Its visa work covers family visas for spouses and children, freelance visas for independent professionals, partner visas, newborn baby visas, single mother visas and parents visas. On the business side, the firm handles trade licence renewal, new business setup, online Ejari registration, labour quota approval and instant licensing. The website says the team puts transparency, efficiency and peace of mind first.

The service suits people applying for a UAE family, freelance or parents visa, and business owners who need a licence renewal, a new company set up or a labour quota. A first enquiry can be made free of charge through social media, and WhatsApp is listed for contact. The office is at Office M03, Sajaya 6, Same Baba Chai Building, Hor Al Anz, Deira. The site invites visitors to call in during normal business hours, which it lists as 09:00 am to 09:00 pm.

What they offer

  • Family Visa: Support for applying for a family visa to bring a spouse and children to live in the UAE. The team handles documentation and approvals.
  • Freelance Visa: Visa assistance for independent professionals such as creatives, consultants and digital nomads. The site describes the support as legal, fast and tailored to independent workers.
  • Renew License: Renewal of a Dubai trade licence, with timely submissions and complete paperwork so a business stays active.
  • New Business Setup: Complete new business setup in the UAE, from trade name registration to final licensing.
  • Online Ejari: Online registration of tenancy contracts with Ejari, processed in line with Dubai Municipality regulations.

Good to know

  • Visits welcome during normal business hours

How do I choose a Golden Visa consultant in Dubai?

Look for a firm that clearly lists Golden Visa support and explains what it will do at each stage. Ask for the scope of work in writing before you agree to anything.

Do PRO services in Dubai submit applications to the government?

Some PRO firms state that they handle submissions with government authorities and follow up until a process is complete. Check each firm's website or ask them directly which steps they cover for your case.

Is a PRO service the same as a government office?

Not necessarily. Some firms, such as PRO Services Dubai, say they operate as independent service providers rather than government entities. Confirm this when you enquire.

What should I ask before hiring a visa consultant in Dubai?

Ask what services are included, who will handle your file and how you will be kept updated. It is also sensible to confirm the fees and expected timelines in writing before you pay.
