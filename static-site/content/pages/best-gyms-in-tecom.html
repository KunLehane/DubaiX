Best Gyms in TECOM

Four gyms in TECOM for strength, classes and flexible training.

4 local options · Updated October 2026

TECOM is one of Dubai's busiest business districts, and a gym close to work or home makes it far easier to keep a routine. We've looked at four options, from CrossFit and kickboxing to 24-hour access and free parking.

The gyms are listed in no particular order. We researched each one from its official website rather than visiting in person, so confirm current details directly with the gym before you go, and use each entry to find the training style that suits you.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. We checked locations, services and contact details against each business's official website in October 2026. We have not visited them in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and availability directly with the business.

Images come from each business's own website, credited below.

Face Off Fitness - Barsha Heights

A good fit for: Varied classes and personal training

Face Off Fitness at Barsha Heights presents itself as a community-driven gym built around real training, consistency and progress. Its personal training is customised to each member's goals, whether that means building muscle, losing weight, increasing strength, boosting endurance or toning. Certified trainers create a structured one-on-one plan for the member's fitness level, and the site also lists functional fitness, mobility and flexibility, and nutritional advice. Group options listed include Zumba, CrossFit and kickboxing.

The gym suits people who want modern equipment, spacious workout areas and supportive coaching to stay committed to their goals. Membership packages are listed at 300 AED+VAT for one month, 825 AED+VAT for three months, 1200 AED+VAT for six months and 1600 AED+VAT for twelve months, each with a Register Now option. The Barsha Heights branch is at Al Shafar Tower 2, TECOM, and the site notes free parking. Enquiries can be made by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

What they offer

  • Personal Training: Customised one-on-one plans from certified trainers, covering goals such as weight loss, muscle building and mobility. The site also mentions nutritional advice.
  • Zumba: Zumba class listed among the gym's programmes.
  • CrossFit Training: CrossFit training is listed among the gym's programmes.
  • Kick Boxing: Kick boxing is listed among the gym's programmes.

Good to know

  • Free parking
  • Certified personal trainers
  • Membership packages from 300 AED+VAT for one month

Google rating: 4.8 out of 5 from 787 reviews (checked October 2026).

Ask about the one to twelve month membership options and whether free parking suits your journey, then message the team on WhatsApp to arrange a visit.

02

Red Gym

A good fit for: Martial arts and group classes

Red Gym offers group classes led by experienced instructors, including karate, boxing, Zumba, MMA and Spin Fit. The site presents the classes as a way to stay engaged and motivated, whether a visitor prefers high-intensity interval training or something gentler. Coaches are listed by speciality, covering CrossFit and fitness, bodybuilding, and fitness and nutrition, and the gym also offers personal training for people working towards specific goals.

The gym suits people looking for group training, one-to-one coaching or a sophisticated space for their own clients. Visitors can come for a tour and a free day pass, or contact the team to learn about membership options. The site does not give detailed booking steps, so enquiries are best made by phone, WhatsApp or email. Red Gym is at Vista Tower on Al Thuraya Street in Barsha Heights, Dubai, and is open daily from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

What they offer

  • Group classes: Karate, boxing, Zumba, MMA and Spin Fit classes led by experienced instructors. The site describes the classes as suited to a range of preferences, from high-intensity training to more relaxed sessions.
  • Personal training: One-to-one training with the gym's coaches, aimed at helping people achieve their fitness goals. Enquiries are handled through the site's contact options.
  • Studio hire: Training space available for freelance personal trainers to train clients or host classes. Interested trainers are asked to contact the team.
  • Free day pass: Visitors can take a tour and a free day pass before deciding on membership. Membership options are available on request.

Good to know

  • Open daily, 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM
  • Free day pass available for visitors
  • Coaches listed for CrossFit, bodybuilding and fitness and nutrition

Google rating: 4.6 out of 5 from 626 reviews (checked October 2026).

Check the daily opening hours, which run from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and ask about the free day pass if you'd like to try it first.

Snap Fitness Media City

A good fit for: Round-the-clock training and app tracking

Snap Fitness Dubai Media City describes itself as a locally owned fitness facility with 24/7 access for members. The gym lists cardio equipment, free weights, strength training and functional training equipment, along with Myzone heart rate training and a body scanner. The site presents its supportive community as a central part of the experience, with the aim of helping members move their body and improve their mood. It suits members who want round-the-clock access and a range of training options.

Visitors can start with a free trial or book a tour, and the site lists a phone number and email for contact. Membership options are shown on the site, with terms and conditions applying and payments described as nonrefundable. Amenities include private showers, private bathrooms and free onsite parking. The gym is on Level 2 of Dubai Jewel Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, in Media City.

What they offer

  • Group Classes: Scheduled classes include yoga, a HIIT workout using barbells, weighted plates and bodyweight exercises, and a high-intensity bootcamp with rotating stations. The yoga class focuses on relaxing the body and reducing stress.
  • Cardio and Strength Equipment: The gym lists cardio equipment, free weights and strength training equipment, alongside functional training equipment. Myzone heart rate training is also offered.
  • Personal Training: Personal training is listed among the gym's amenities. The website does not give further details of the service.
  • Snap App: The free Snap App is personalised to members' goals, with progress tracking, on-demand workouts, personalised meal plans and mental health programmes.

Good to know

  • Open 24 hours to members
  • Free onsite parking
  • Free trial available

Google rating: 4.6 out of 5 from 566 reviews (checked October 2026).

If your schedule is unpredictable, the 24-hour access and free trial make it easy to test the gym at a time that suits you.

Fitness Avenue

A good fit for: Serious strength and conditioning training

Fitness Avenue describes itself as a focused, comfortable gym built around strength and conditioning. The training floor offers modern strength and cardio equipment, cable and rig stations and an abdominal area. The site says the gym suits people who want deliberate, consistent work rather than gimmicks. The doors open at 6AM and members have 18 hours of daily access, and between sessions they can refuel at the club bar.

Memberships are available monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annually, with prices in AED that include 5% VAT. Every plan includes full access to the training floor and free parking at Warsan Tower. Prospective members can call, message on WhatsApp or send an enquiry form, and the gym says it replies within 24 hours. Esaad card holders are welcome. The gym is located at 1st Floor, Warsan Tower 10, Al Rabee'a 2, Barsha Heights, Dubai.

What they offer

  • Strength Floor: A training floor with modern strength equipment, cable and rig stations. The site describes it as built for consistency and deliberate work.
  • Cardio Deck: A dedicated cardio area with modern equipment, where members can pick their own pace.
  • Abdomen Area: A dedicated space for abdominal training on the main floor.
  • Flexible Memberships: Monthly, quarterly, half-year and annual plans, each with full training floor access and free parking at Warsan Tower. Tabby and Tamara instalment options are offered, subject to approval.
  • Club Bar: A bar on site where members can refuel between sessions.

Good to know

  • Doors open at 6AM, with 18 hours of daily access
  • Free parking for members at Warsan Tower
  • Esaad card holders welcome
  • Enquiries answered within 24 hours

Google rating: 4.3 out of 5 from 123 reviews (checked October 2026).

Compare the monthly and yearly membership choices and confirm the free parking at Warsan Tower before you sign up.

Which gyms in TECOM offer personal training?

Face Off Fitness Barsha Heights and Red Gym both mention certified trainers, and Snap Fitness Media City also lists personal training. Fitness Avenue is focused on its strength floor, so contact the gym directly to ask about one-to-one sessions.

Is there a 24-hour gym in TECOM?

Snap Fitness Dubai Media City is listed as a 24-hour gym on Level 2 of Dubai Jewel Tower. Opening arrangements can change, so confirm the current schedule with the gym before visiting.

Do TECOM gyms offer free parking?

Face Off Fitness Barsha Heights lists free parking, and Fitness Avenue offers free parking at Warsan Tower for members. Check that parking is available for your visit before you travel.

Can I try a gym in TECOM before joining?

Red Gym offers a free day pass for visitors, and Snap Fitness Media City has a free trial. Confirm the current terms with each gym before you arrive.
