Best Gyms in Al Barsha

Six gyms in Al Barsha and Barsha Heights, from 24 hour floors to a women-only club.

6 local options · Updated October 2026

Al Barsha has a good spread of gyms, from bodybuilding floors and kickboxing classes to a women-only club with recovery suites. This guide brings together six of them, listed in no particular order.

We researched each gym from its official website and have not tested them in person, so use the details as a starting point. Look at the services and hours, then check prices, availability and facilities with the gym directly before you sign up.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. Are there 24 hour gyms in Al Barsha?

Based on their websites, Fat to Fit Fitness Gym and Body Master Gym both list round-the-clock access. It is worth confirming hours with the gym before you visit.

Is there a women-only gym in Al Barsha?

Warehouse Gym Al Barsha Ladies is a dedicated women's gym on Level 2 of Grove Mall. Its website mentions private changing rooms and recovery facilities.

Can I try a gym in Al Barsha before joining?

Some gyms make this easy: RedFit Gym lists a free day pass and tour, and Warehouse Gym Al Barsha Ladies offers day and week passes. Others, such as Body Master Gym, offer a free consultation.

What should I check before joining a gym in Al Barsha?

Look at opening hours, the classes or training styles on offer, and whether lockers, showers and parking are included. Ask about membership length and any trial options before you commit.
