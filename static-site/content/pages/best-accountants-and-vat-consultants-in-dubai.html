Do I need a VAT consultant in Dubai?

Not every business needs one, but a consultant can help with VAT registration, returns and record keeping. Whether it is worth it depends on your turnover, the activities you carry out and how confident you feel handling the rules yourself.

What does a business accountant in Dubai do?

A business accountant typically manages bookkeeping, financial statements, tax filings and, for some firms, audit support. Many also advise on VAT and corporate tax so that records stay in order.

Can a company setup firm also handle accounting?

Many business setup firms also offer accounting, VAT or corporate tax services, although the range differs from firm to firm. Check the services listed on their website or ask them directly before you commit.

How do I choose an accountant or VAT consultant in Dubai?

Start by listing the services you need, such as bookkeeping, VAT returns or corporate tax, and compare which firms cover them. Ask about experience, how they communicate and whether they offer a consultation before you sign anything.
