Best Bespoke Tailors for Women in Dubai

Six Dubai studios for suits, evening wear, bridal pieces and alterations, compared by what each does best.

6 local options · Updated November 2026

Dubai has a strong tradition of made-to-measure tailoring, and several studios now focus on women's garments, from sharp business suits to evening gowns. Finding the right tailor depends on what you need, whether that is a fitted blazer, a wedding outfit or a considered alteration.

The six tailors below are listed in no particular order. We researched each one from its official website and have not visited the studios in person, so check opening hours, services and appointment options directly before you go.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. We checked locations, services and contact details against each business's official website in November 2026. We have not visited them in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and availability directly with the business.

A good fit for: Made-to-measure garments and weddings

Suited and Booted is a bespoke tailoring business with stores in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. The website presents luxury bespoke tailoring alongside ready-to-wear collections, and lists dedicated sections for casual wear, tuxedos, weddings and women's garments. Each piece is described as made-to-measure, reflecting the client's own taste and style, and the team includes style consultants and experienced tailors. The business also publishes a lookbook for occasion and wardrobe ideas.

Booking can be made through the website, WhatsApp, email or phone. The process begins with an initial consultation covering preferences, requirements, location, climate and occasion. Clients then attend three fitting sessions with Style Consultants and Master Tailors before collecting the finished garment. The Dubai store is at Goshi Warehouse City, Warehouse 8, 19D Street, Al Quoz.

Libas Legacy

A good fit for: Family tailoring house with a long history

Libas Legacy describes itself as a family tailoring house founded in 1992 by MD. Azad. Its women's bespoke tailoring focuses on individually commissioned garments developed around measurements, silhouette, fabric, personal style and occasion, rather than a predetermined retail size. The custom tailors are trained in business tailoring and formalwear, wedding clothing and everyday tailored garments. The business also offers garment alterations for existing clothing that needs reshaping, shortening or fit correction.

A commission starts with a personal consultation, where the garment, occasion, preferred style and fit expectations are discussed. Measurements and proportions follow, then fabric and design selection, with advice on Dubai's climate. Cutting and construction are followed by a basted fitting and a final fitting before delivery. Clients who cannot visit in person can use the remote suit service. The showroom is at 1229, Tamani Arts Building on Al Asayel Street in Business Bay, and it is open 10 am to 10 pm, Monday to Friday and Sunday.

What they offer

  • Women's Bespoke Tailoring: Individually commissioned garments developed around measurements, silhouette, fabric, personal style and occasion. Clothing is created around the individual wearer rather than a predetermined retail size.
  • Bespoke Suits: Suits created around individual measurements, selected fabric, preferred silhouette and intended use. Suits are made for business, formal occasions, weddings and personal wardrobes.
  • Wedding Tailoring: Wedding clothing that considers fabric, formality, styling, proportions and fitting requirements from the start of the commission.
  • Garment Alterations: Professional alterations for clothing that needs reshaping, shortening, fit correction or further refinement.

Good to know

How does bespoke tailoring work for women in Dubai?

Bespoke tailoring usually starts with a consultation to discuss your measurements, style and fabric, followed by one or more fittings before the garment is finished. Many studios follow a structured process from consultation to delivery, so ask about the timeline before you commit.

Can I get an evening gown or wedding outfit made in Dubai?

Several of the tailors in this guide offer evening gowns, bridal wear or wedding garments. Check each studio's services and book a consultation to talk through your design.

Do bespoke tailors in Dubai offer home visits?

Some do, including House of Tailors, Belissimo Bespoke and The Bespoke Corner, which offer home or office visits or consultations. Contact the studio directly to confirm availability for your area and dates.

How long does a bespoke garment take to make?

The time needed depends on the garment, the fabric and the number of fittings, so it varies from studio to studio. Ask for an estimated timeline at your first consultation so you can plan ahead.

Can women's tailors in Dubai alter ready-to-wear clothing?

Several studios in this guide offer alterations, including Libas Legacy, Belissimo Bespoke, House of Tailors and Gianna N' Giacca. Discuss the fit and the work needed with the team before you bring in the garment.
