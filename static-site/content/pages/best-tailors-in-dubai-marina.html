Best Tailors in Dubai Marina

Five tailors in Dubai Marina, from bespoke suits to quick alterations.

5 local options · Updated November 2026

Dubai Marina has a wide range of tailors, from long-established bespoke houses to neighbourhood studios that handle everyday alterations. Whether you need a suit for a wedding, a dress taken in before an event, or a shirt made to measure, there is a studio to suit the job.

This guide covers five tailors in the Marina, listed in no particular order. Each entry notes what the business is best known for and offers a practical tip for your first visit. We researched them from their official websites and have not tested them in person, so check current details directly before you go.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. We checked locations, services and contact details against each business's official website in November 2026. We have not visited them in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and availability directly with the business.

My Tailor & Little Things

A good fit for: Everyday alterations and home visits

The business describes itself as a tailoring service that handles everything from simple alterations, such as shortening, hemming and tapering, to major modifications. Its services include bespoke suits, shirts, gowns and dresses, and ethnic wear, as well as repairs, zip replacements and uniforms. According to the site, bespoke work starts with a consultation and a choice of fabrics and details such as lapels, collars and cuffs, followed by detailed measurements.

Customers can visit one of the stores in Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Lake Towers or DIFC, or book a visiting tailor who comes to a home or office. The site says the team includes female staff who take measurements, which may suit women who prefer that. Appointments can be requested through the website, and the price list gives published rates for many alteration jobs, though some are shown as ranges. The Dubai Marina store number is +971 4 557 9495.

What they offer

  • Alterations: Shortening, hemming, tapering and resizing for women and men. The published price list covers trousers, jackets, skirts, dresses and other garments.
  • Bespoke Suits: Customers choose fabric and style details such as lapels, collars and cuffs, then have their measurements taken. The service also extends to jackets, waistcoats, trousers and shirts.
  • Dress Making: Custom dresses made from a selection of designs and fabrics, including maxi dresses, gowns, wedding gowns and abayas.
  • Home Tailor Visit: A visiting tailor service at a home or office for bespoke suits, evening dresses or a batch of alterations.
  • Repairs and Little Things: Repairs, zip replacements, button fixing, cushion stitching and uniforms, according to the site's list of services.

Good to know

  • Three locations: Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Lake Towers and DIFC
  • Home and office tailor visits
  • Female staff take measurements
  • Tailoring business established in 2013

Google rating: 4.9 out of 5 from 726 reviews (checked November 2026).

The Sewing Corner

A good fit for: Bridal alterations and ladies' tailoring

The Sewing Corner offers alterations and tailoring for both womenswear and menswear. Its listed services include standard alterations, formalwear alterations, bridal alterations, custom tailoring for ladies and men, and soft furnishing alterations. Selected alterations can be completed within the same day, and the website states that quality craftsmanship and dependable service have earned the trust of customers.

The business suits people who want a female tailor, those who cannot easily get to a shop and would like home pickup and drop off, and customers who need a quicker turnaround. The Dubai Marina branch is at Marina Walk on Al Tarres Street, next to Starbucks and opposite Marks & Spencer Food, and is open daily from 10 AM to 10 PM. Customers can book an appointment through the website or contact the branch by WhatsApp.

What they offer

  • Standard Alterations: Alteration work for everyday garments.
  • Bridal Alterations: Alteration work for bridal garments.
  • Custom Tailoring for Ladies: Bespoke tailoring for womenswear, with female tailors available at every branch.
  • Soft Furnishing Alterations: Alteration work for soft furnishings.

Good to know

  • Female tailors available at every branch
  • Home pickup and drop off service
  • Express 1 day service for selected alterations
  • Open daily, 10 AM to 10 PM

Santoba Tailors & Co

A good fit for: Corporate suits and wedding suits

Santoba Tailors & Co offers bespoke and made-to-measure tailoring. Services listed on the website include corporate suits, handcrafted wedding suits, shirts and trousers, women's suits, alterations to existing garments and smart casual pieces. The business also offers a curated menswear accessories range, including ties, pocket squares, cufflinks, chinos, footwear and sunglasses. It suits clients who want a garment fitted to them and those who need a suit for work, a wedding or a tailored finish to an existing item.

A visit typically begins with a consultation, which can be booked in advance or arranged by walking in to a showroom. Fittings take place in stages: a trial fitting assesses measurements and fit, with adjustments made as needed, and a final fitting checks the suit before most clients collect it. The website lists a Dubai Marina location at The Marina Torch. Payment can be made by cash, card, US dollars, euros or sterling pounds.

What they offer

  • Corporate Suits: Tailored suits for work, described on the website as sharp corporate suits. Made-to-measure fittings are arranged in stages.
  • Bespoke Wedding Suits: Handcrafted suits for weddings. The website notes that the team discusses style preferences and the wedding vision before designing the garment.
  • Shirts & Trousers: Made-to-measure shirts and trousers listed as a separate service on the website.
  • Women's Suits: Tailored suits for women, listed as a dedicated service on the website.
  • Alterations: Expert alteration of existing garments so that ready-made or ill-fitting clothing can be adjusted.

Good to know

  • Bespoke garments delivered in about 2 weeks, with rush services available
  • Multiple fitting sessions for precise adjustments
  • Express service available for an additional charge, with a 5-7 day turnaround
  • Cash, card, US dollar, euro and sterling pound payments accepted

Royal Ascot Windsor

A good fit for: Men's bespoke suits and tuxedos

Royal Ascot Windsor offers bespoke and made-to-measure tailoring for men. Services include custom suits, custom shirts, wedding suits, tuxedos and alterations, with cloth drawn from mills such as Loro Piana, Scabal and Ermenegildo Zegna. Garments are hand-finished by master tailors, according to the site. It is aimed at men who value fit, style and detail.

A visit begins with a free consultation, which can be booked online or through WhatsApp. The site describes a five-stage process from consultation to final press, with measurements logged to a personal chart and fittings checked on the body. The atelier is at Marina Diamond 1, Dubai Marina, open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, with Sunday by appointment. Doorstep tailoring covers Dubai Marina, JBR, JLT, Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills and other districts.

What they offer

  • Bespoke Suits: Built from a personal block and paper pattern, then hand-cut for a fit the site says no rack can offer.
  • Custom Suits: Made-to-measure tailoring with full control over cloth, canvas, lapel and lining.
  • Custom Shirts: Egyptian and Italian cottons, cut to the collar, cuff and monogram of your choosing.
  • Wedding Suits: Looks for the groom, groomsmen and reception, tailored around your date.
  • Alterations: Shoulder, waist, sleeve and hem work for garments that deserve a second life.

Good to know

  • Free consultation
  • Open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM
  • Doorstep tailoring across Dubai

Google rating: 4.9 out of 5 from 133 reviews (checked November 2026).

Kachins Couture

A good fit for: Hand-fitted bespoke suits and shirts

Kachins Couture offers bespoke tailoring with a focus on suits and formal wear. Its services include bespoke suits, blazers, waistcoats, shirts, trousers, shorts and tuxedos. The website describes fabric sourced directly from mills in Italy and England, with certified origin, and a range of crease-resistant travel blends. Each order begins with a consultation covering the client's style, lifestyle and the occasion behind the garment.

The business suits clients who want a garment made around their own measurements and who value a considered fitting process. A visit typically moves through fabric and detail selection, measurement and pattern cutting, then two in-person fittings, with a third available on request. Finished garments can be collected or delivered to the client's home. The Dubai Marina branch is at Shop no.3, Tamani Hotel, Al Sufouh Road, and is open Monday to Sunday, 11am to 8pm. Appointments can be booked through the website.

What they offer

  • Bespoke Suits: Suits made to measure, with a pattern drafted around the client's posture, frame and stance. The site highlights fine Italian fabrics for the collection.
  • Bespoke Shirts: Made-to-measure shirts built from the same measurement and pattern process used for the business's other garments.
  • Bespoke Tuxedos: Tailored tuxedos made to order, listed alongside suits, blazers and waistcoats in the bespoke range.
  • Home and Office Measurement: Measurement visits at the client's home or office, at a time that suits them, across the UAE.
  • Hand Fitting: Physical draft and fit sessions, with multiple fittings to correct drape and adjust the silhouette for ease of movement.

Good to know

  • Open Monday to Sunday, 11am to 8pm
  • Home and office measurements across the UAE
  • Tailoring since 1981

How do I choose a tailor in Dubai Marina?

Start with the type of work you need, such as alterations, made-to-measure suits or formal wear, and look for a tailor who specialises in it. Check whether the studio offers a consultation or home visit if that suits you better.

Do tailors in Dubai Marina offer home visits?

Several tailors in the area offer home or office visits, which can save time if your schedule is busy. Check each business's website or contact them directly to confirm what is available and where they cover.

Which Dubai Marina tailors make wedding suits?

Some of the tailors in this guide list wedding suits or bridal alterations among their services. Ask about lead times early, especially if your wedding is close, so there is time for fittings.
