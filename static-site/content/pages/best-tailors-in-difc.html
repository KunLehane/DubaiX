Stallion Bespoke

A good fit for: Made-to-measure suits and wedding tailoring

Stallion Bespoke is a bespoke tailor with four boutiques across Dubai, founded in 2014 and backed by the Shree Group, whose textile business began in 1971. The studio makes suits, shirts, jackets and trousers, along with wedding tailoring, corporate wardrobe solutions, VIP tailoring delivered to a home, office or hotel, and alterations. Measurements cover more than thirty points, and the cloth comes from mills in England and Italy.

The service suits business leaders, entrepreneurs, executives, diplomats, lawyers, bankers and creative professionals. A visit starts with a consultation about profession, lifestyle and the purpose of the garment, followed by fabric and design choices, measurements, a basted draft fitting and final refinements. Appointments can be requested through the website or by WhatsApp, with confirmation promised within a few hours, and consultations are not charged. The Downtown Dubai boutique is at Tower 3, South Ridge Park, in the Burj Khalifa area.

What they offer

  • Bespoke Suits: Fully personalised suits tailored exclusively to your measurements, lifestyle and personal style.
  • Bespoke Shirts: Custom shirts crafted from premium cottons and linens with refined detailing throughout.
  • Bespoke Jackets: Versatile jackets designed for business, travel and leisure, balancing precision and elegance.
  • Wedding Tailoring: Custom garments for grooms, groomsmen, wedding parties and formal occasions with distinction.
  • Refinement Services: Expert alterations and garment refinements executed with exceptional precision and care throughout.

Good to know

  • Four boutiques across Dubai
  • Master tailors on site at every location
  • Consultations are free
  • Lifetime alteration support

Collars & Cuffs

A good fit for: Home and office suit appointments

Collars & Cuffs offers bespoke tailoring for men who want clothing cut for them alone. The process starts with measurements, posture and style, after which each piece is crafted from premium fabrics by mills such as Ermenegildo Zegna and Scabal, with the fit refined across personal fittings. The range covers business suits, wedding tuxedos, everyday shirts and trousers, and the site lists a collection of over 5,000 fabrics.

The business suits clients who value a personal approach and busy schedules, as the concierge provides home and office appointments in Dubai for connoisseur members. A visit begins with a consultation, which can be booked through the website form or by phone, with a preferred date and time selected. The showroom is in Unit 014, Gate Avenue Mall, Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai.

What they offer

  • Made-to-measure suits and shirts: Bespoke suits, shirts, trousers and tuxedos are made from your measurements, posture and style. The fit is refined across personal fittings until it is right.
  • Home and office appointments: A personal concierge provides appointments at home or in the office for connoisseur members. This is offered to suit busy schedules.
  • Express service: The business offers urgent bespoke suits for unforeseen events, according to the website.
  • Wedding suits: Wedding suits and wedding tuxedos are offered as part of the bespoke range.
  • Accessories and leather goods: The range includes pocket squares, cufflinks, ties and leather goods.

Good to know

  • Complimentary home and office appointments for loyalty programme members
  • Collection of over 5,000 fabrics
  • Express service for urgent bespoke suits
  • Four generations of bespoke tailoring

House of Tailors

A good fit for: Women's bespoke suits and linen

House of Tailors makes custom clothing for men and women, including bespoke and made-to-measure suits, linen suits, wedding suits and winter suits. The site describes a library of over 5,000 fabrics held in store, sourced from mills in Switzerland, the UK and Italy. It suits clients who want a garment shaped to their own build, style and purpose.

A visit starts with a consultation at the Business Bay studio in Fifty One Tower, Marasi Drive, or remotely for clients elsewhere. The process includes precision measurements, a personal pattern and typically two to three fittings. Made-to-measure delivery typically takes 10 to 14 days, and bespoke suits three to four weeks. Home visits, virtual appointments and WhatsApp contact are also offered.

What they offer

  • Men's bespoke and made-to-measure suits: Custom suits for men, including bespoke suits, double-breasted suits and tuxedos. Measurements go beyond standard sizing and are used to draft a personal pattern.
  • Women's bespoke suits: Bespoke suits for women, including women's business suits and occasion suits. Clients can choose the fabric, lapels, pockets, lining and buttons.
  • Linen suits: Custom-tailored linen suits, along with linen shirts, trousers and jackets for warm weather.
  • Wedding suits: Bespoke wedding suits for men and women, designed for the wedding and other special events.

Good to know

  • Over 5,000 fabrics in store
  • Two to three fittings in the bespoke process
  • Made-to-measure delivery typically 10 to 14 days
  • Home visits and virtual appointments available

A good fit for: Alterations, dresses and ethnic wear

The business describes itself as a one-stop shop for custom sartorial needs, crafting each garment from a unique pattern after a tailoring consultation and hand-finishing it. Clients can choose from a range of fabrics and design options, and the team handles everything from simple alterations such as shortening, hemming and tapering to larger modifications. Women's dressmaking, ethnic wear, costumes and embroidery are also listed among its services.

A typical bespoke order starts with a consultation on fabric, style and fit details such as lapels, collars, cuffs and vents, followed by detailed body measurements from the tailors. Professional female staff are available to take measurements. Clients who prefer not to visit a store can book a home or office visit. The DIFC branch sits alongside the company's other locations in Dubai Marina and Jumeirah Lake Towers.

What they offer

  • Home Visiting Tailor Service: Tailoring at your home or office, covering bespoke suits, evening dresses and alterations, arranged by appointment.

How do I choose a tailor in DIFC?

Start with what you need, whether that is a bespoke suit, a wedding outfit or simple alterations, and match it to the studio's services. Then check the location, booking options and whether a consultation is included before you commit.

Do DIFC tailors offer home appointments?

Some do, including Collars & Cuffs, which offers home and office appointments, and My Tailor & Little Things, which has a home visiting service. Check with each studio before booking, as availability can vary.

Can I get a wedding suit made in DIFC?

Several tailors in this guide offer wedding suits, including Stallion Bespoke, Collars & Cuffs, House of Tailors and Bespoke Men's Tailor. Book a consultation early so there is time for fittings before the big day.

Do I need to book a tailor in advance?

Most bespoke work involves more than one fitting, so booking ahead helps you stay on schedule. For simple alterations you may be able to visit without an appointment, but check with the studio first.

Are all of these tailors inside DIFC?

Most are, but House of Tailors works from Business Bay, and Stallion Bespoke's Downtown Dubai studio is nearby rather than inside DIFC. Confirm the address on each website before you visit.
