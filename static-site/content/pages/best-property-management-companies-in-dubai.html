What does a property management company do in Dubai?

A property management company looks after a home or investment on behalf of its owner, which can include handling tenants, arranging maintenance and keeping the owner informed. The exact services differ from company to company, so check what is included before you agree to anything.

How do I choose a property manager in Dubai?

Start by confirming which services are included and which are charged separately. Then ask how they communicate with owners and how they handle maintenance requests.

Is holiday home management different from long-term property management?

Holiday home services usually focus on short stays, including bookings and guest turnover, while long-term management centres on tenancies. Some companies offer both, so ask which applies to your property.

Can I use a property management company if I live abroad?

Many owners who live overseas use a manager to handle day-to-day matters at their Dubai property. Ask how each company reports to owners and how you will be kept updated.
