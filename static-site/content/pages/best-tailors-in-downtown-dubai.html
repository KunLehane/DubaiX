Tailoring in central Dubai

Best Tailors in Downtown Dubai

Six tailors in and around Downtown, from bespoke suits to wedding wear and alterations

6 local options · Updated November 2026

Dubai has plenty of tailors, and the choice can be hard when you need a suit for a wedding or a shirt that fits properly. This guide brings together six studios in and around Downtown Dubai, researched from their official websites.

Not every studio is inside Downtown itself. Two are in Downtown, and the other four are in Business Bay and DIFC, so check the address before you visit. The list is not ranked, and each entry notes what the tailor suits best along with a practical tip for a first visit.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. We checked locations, services and contact details against each business's official website in November 2026. We have not visited them in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and availability directly with the business.

Stallion Bespoke

A good fit for: Full bespoke suits and corporate wardrobes

Stallion Bespoke offers bespoke suits, shirts, jackets and trousers, along with wedding tailoring, corporate tailoring, VIP tailoring delivered to a home, office or hotel, and refinement services such as alterations. The business was established in 2014 and is backed by the Shree Group, whose textile journey began in 1971. Its garments are made from cloth sourced from mills in England and Italy. It suits business leaders, entrepreneurs, executives, lawyers, bankers and creative professionals who want a garment designed around them rather than selected from standard sizing.

A visit begins with a consultation covering your profession, lifestyle and the purpose of the garment. Over 30 individual measurements are taken, followed by a basted fitting, a refinement stage and handover, after which the pattern is saved for future orders. Consultations are not charged. Appointments can be requested through the website, with confirmation on WhatsApp within a few hours.

What they offer

  • Bespoke Suits: Suits tailored to your measurements, lifestyle and personal style. Each suit is made exclusively for the individual wearing it.
  • Bespoke Shirts: Custom shirts crafted from premium cottons and linens, with refined detailing throughout.
  • Wedding Tailoring: Custom garments for grooms, groomsmen, wedding parties and formal occasions.
  • Corporate Tailoring: Wardrobe solutions for executives, entrepreneurs, professionals and organisations.
  • Refinement Services: Expert alterations and garment refinements carried out with precision and care. The site states that lifetime alteration support is included after handover.

Good to know

  • Four boutiques across Dubai
  • Master tailors on site at every location
  • Consultations are not charged

Google rating: 4.9 out of 5 from 732 reviews (checked November 2026).

House of Tailors

A good fit for: Linen, winter and womenswear suits

House of Tailors offers custom suits and tailored clothing for men and women. Its range covers bespoke suits, made-to-measure suits, double-breasted suits, tuxedos, linen suits, winter suits and wedding suits. Womenswear includes bespoke suits, business suits, formal dresses and abayas, and accessories such as ties, cufflinks and pocket squares are also listed. The business says its stylists, designers and tailors help clients choose fabrics, lapels, linings and buttons.

Clients can book an in-store consultation or a video appointment, or arrange a home or office visit with a stylist. The bespoke process runs from consultation and fabric selection through measurements, pattern drafting and one to three fittings, ending with final delivery. The site says bespoke orders typically take three to four weeks, while made-to-measure delivery is typically 10 to 14 days. The Dubai studio is at Suite 710, Fifty One Tower in Business Bay, and appointments can also be booked through the website, WhatsApp or Instagram.

What they offer

  • Bespoke suits: Custom-made suits tailored to the client's style, preferences and lifestyle, with fabric and design details chosen step by step. Typical delivery is three to four weeks.
  • Made-to-measure suits: Suits made to the client's measurements, with delivery typically in 10 to 14 days.
  • Linen suits: Custom-tailored linen suits, along with linen shirts and linen trousers, for hot weather.
  • Wedding suits: Tailored suits for weddings and special events, including bespoke wedding suits for grooms.
  • Womenswear: Bespoke suits for women, business suits, formal dresses and abayas, tailored to individual style.

Good to know

  • Over 5,000 fabrics in-store
  • Home and office visits by a stylist
  • Made-to-measure delivery typically 10 to 14 days
  • Fabrics from Switzerland, the UK and Italy

Libas Legacy

A good fit for: Family-run commissions and safari suits

Libas Legacy offers bespoke tailoring for men and women, with individually commissioned garments developed through personal consultation, precise measurements, fabric selection, fittings and refinement. The family tailoring house was founded by MD. Azad in 1992 and says it combines more than three decades of experience with a personal approach to fit, fabric and style. Services cover business clothing, suits, shirts, trousers, wedding wear, safari suits, uniforms and garment alterations.

Each commission starts with the client's measurements, proportions, preferences, lifestyle and occasion, and the team says it designs around the individual wearer rather than a predetermined retail size. The commission process moves through consultation, cutting, a basted fitting that allows adjustments before completion, and a final fit assessment. Clients who cannot visit in person can use the remote suit service. The business is at 1229 Tamani Arts Building on Al Asayel Street in Business Bay, and its website lists opening hours of 10 am to 10 pm, Monday to Friday and Sunday.

What they offer

  • Bespoke Suits: Suits are created around individual measurements, selected fabric, preferred silhouette and intended use. The business tailors suits for business, formal occasions, weddings and personal wardrobes.
  • Custom Shirts: Shirts are made from individual measurements with the client's preferred cloth, collar, cuffs and fit. They are offered for business, formal and everyday wardrobes.
  • Tailored Trousers: Trousers are developed around waist, rise, length, proportion and fabric drape to create comfort and a cleaner trouser line.
  • Wedding Tailoring: Wedding clothing takes account of fabric, formality, styling, proportions and fitting requirements from the start of the commission. Garments are considered in relation to the venue, dress code and event date.
  • Garment Alterations: Existing garments can be reshaped, shortened or refined for fit correction. The service is intended for clothing that needs refinement rather than replacement.

Good to know

  • Basted fitting before final completion

The Bespoke Corner

A good fit for: Traditional dress and women's tailoring

The Bespoke Corner offers suits, tuxedos and traditional dress, each made to the wearer's measurements. The process starts with a consultation, where the team discusses what is needed and takes measurements. Clients then choose from more than 3,000 fabrics from brands including Loro Piana, Zegna and Scabal. A paper pattern is drawn for each client, the fabric is cut by hand, and the piece is fitted and adjusted until it is right before final stitching and pressing.

The business suits clients looking for bespoke tailoring for occasions such as black tie events, work and meetings, and weddings, and it also tailors for women. Bookings start on WhatsApp. Visits can be made to the showroom in Shop 4, Ground Floor, Damac Majestine Tower, Business Bay, open Saturday to Thursday from 11:00 to 20:00 and closed on Fridays, or the team can come to a home or office anywhere in Dubai.

What they offer

How long does bespoke tailoring take in Dubai?

Timelines vary between studios and depend on how many fittings a garment needs. Ask each tailor for their expected schedule at the consultation so you can plan around any events.

What should I bring to a tailor fitting?

Bring the shoes and shirt you plan to wear with the garment, along with any reference images you like. Being clear about the occasion helps the tailor choose the right cut and fabric.

Do Dubai tailors offer home visits?

Some do, including House of Tailors and The Bespoke Corner, which both offer fittings away from the studio. Check directly with each tailor, as availability can change.

What is the difference between bespoke and made-to-measure?

Bespoke garments are made from a pattern drawn specifically for you, while made-to-measure garments start from an existing pattern adjusted to your measurements. Ask the tailor which method they use before you order.

Can tailors in Dubai do alterations?

Several tailors on this list offer alterations, including Lapels Bespoke Tailors and Libas Legacy. Bring the garment to your appointment so the tailor can assess the fit properly.
