Best Gyms in JVC

Four gyms in Jumeirah Village Circle, from EMS studios to Hyrox training clubs.

4 local options · Updated October 2026

Jumeirah Village Circle has a growing mix of gyms, from high-end branches and EMS studios to group training clubs and hotel gyms. Choosing between them can be hard when each one promises something different. This guide narrows the field to four options worth a look.

The gyms below are listed in no particular order. We researched each one using its official website and have not visited or tested them in person, so check details directly with the gym before you go. Each entry explains what the gym is best for and offers a practical tip to help you decide.

How we chose these

Prime Fitness JVC

A good fit for: Premium equipment and flexible memberships

Prime Fitness operates gyms in JVC, DIP, Marina and Meydan, with two JVC branches at Damac Tower 108 and Sandhurst. The site describes professional coaching, premium equipment and a community focused on training, and it highlights more than 3,000 active members and 14 or more certified trainers. Services listed include personal training, PT packages, classes and memberships, and a VIP home service is also offered.

The business suits people who want structured training with coaching support. Members quoted on the site mention clean facilities, modern equipment and friendly staff. To visit, prospective members can claim a free trial pass at the JVC-Sandhurst, DIP and Marina Waterfront branches, and can book a class through the website. Membership prices start from AED 217 a month at some branches. The JVC branches can be reached by phone or by email at info@primefitnessdxb.ae.

What they offer

  • Personal Training: The site promotes professional coaching from certified trainers. Personal training is listed as a main service.
  • PT Packages: Personal training packages are listed in their own section of the website.
  • Memberships: Memberships are offered at each branch, with monthly prices shown from AED 217 at some locations. Three-month and 30-day options are also advertised.
  • Classes: Members can book classes through the website. The site describes a community that pushes members to train.
  • VIP Home Service: The website lists a VIP home service as one of its offerings. Further details are not shown on the page.

Good to know

  • Google rating of 4.8
  • 14+ certified trainers
  • Free trial pass at JVC-Sandhurst

Google rating: 4.5 out of 5 from 481 reviews (checked October 2026).

Check which JVC branch, at Damac Tower 108 or Sandhurst, is closest to home before comparing membership options.

My30Minutes EMS Gym JVC

A good fit for: Quick EMS sessions with trainer guidance

My30Minutes offers personalised EMS fitness training, where electrical muscle stimulation is combined with exercise monitored by a personal trainer. The site describes the format as 20 minutes of EMS training plus 10 minutes to get in and out of the studio, aimed at people with busy schedules. Stated aims include fast weight loss, body sculpting, increased metabolism and fat reduction.

To book, visitors choose a branch and a trial type on the website. A private trial is listed at 100 AED, with 50 AED payable now, and a duo trial at 50 AED, with 25 AED payable now. Payment is taken by card through Stripe, and a coupon code is emailed for redemption at the studio. Prepaid trials must be used within 30 days and are non-refundable. The JVC studio is listed as open Monday to Sunday, 11am to 10pm.

What they offer

  • EMS personal training: Electrical muscle stimulation sessions monitored by a personal trainer. The site describes each workout as 20 minutes long.
  • Private trial: A trial session listed at 100 AED, with 50 AED payable online before the visit.
  • Duo trial: A trial session listed at 50 AED, with 25 AED payable online before the visit.
  • Home service: A trainer travels to a preferred location with the necessary equipment. The site notes that only limited slots are available and that a little space is needed.

Good to know

  • Open Monday to Sunday, 11am to 10pm
  • Private and duo trials available
  • Home service available in limited slots

Google rating: 4.9 out of 5 from 254 reviews (checked October 2026).

Circuit Fit

A good fit for: Group workouts and Hyrox preparation

Circuit Fit describes its signature Circuit Training as a 60-minute session of stations, with each class led by expert trainers and designed to work the whole body. Participants set their own intensity and load at each station, which the club says suits both beginners and experienced exercisers. Yoga classes focus on mindful flow and flexibility, while Pilates sessions aim to sculpt, align and strengthen. The club states that classes can be scaled, and trainers provide modifications for every exercise.

Booking is handled through the Circuit Fit app, where members can book classes, choose favourite trainers and manage memberships. Class passes range from a 5-day unlimited trial at AED 99 to a 20 Class Pass at AED 1400, and unlimited memberships run for 3 months, 6 months or 1 year. Personal training and small group options are arranged by WhatsApp, and the club recommends bringing athletic wear, clean training shoes, a water bottle and a towel. The studio is at Binghatti Onyx in Al Barsha South Fourth, Jumeirah Village Circle.

What they offer

  • Circuit Training: A signature high-energy group class that mixes strength, cardio and functional movements in a fast-paced circuit. Participants rotate through stations designed to target different muscle groups.
  • Hyrox Simulations: Classes that replicate the official HYROX race format, combining running with functional workout stations. They are aimed at people preparing for the race.
  • Yoga and Pilates: Separate yoga classes focus on mindful flow, flexibility and balance, while pilates sessions aim to build core strength. Both are offered as separate classes to suit different goals.
  • Personal Training: One-to-one sessions or small group training for groups of four, arranged by WhatsApp. Mixed and women-only options are available.
  • Kids Classes: Circuit Training and yoga classes for children over 6 years of age, run in a fun group setting. Coaches focus on correct form and the safety of young participants.

Good to know

  • Official HYROX Training Club
  • Women-only options available
  • Kids classes for children over 6
  • 5-day unlimited trial for AED 99

Google rating: 4.9 out of 5 from 219 reviews (checked October 2026).

If you are training for a HYROX race, ask about the official training club programme before you join.

Move at FIVE

A good fit for: Hotel gym with recovery facilities

Move at FIVE is the fitness space at FIVE Jumeirah Village, offering an indoor gym, personal training, classes, a wellness zone and day passes. The site describes it as a hotel gym where fitness experts are on hand to help members reach their body goals, whether they want to push their limits or relax. Group Strength Classes run on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and HYROX-style sessions are also offered. It suits people who want guided training or a place to recover after a workout.

A Gym Day Pass costs AED120 and includes indoor gym access, one class, steam and sauna access and complimentary valet service. An ice bath can be added for AED55, and a body scan and assessment costs AED100. Personal training starts from AED320 per session. The gym is located at Dubai District 14, Street 1, Jumeirah Village Circle, and is open daily from 5 AM to 10 PM. Enquiries can be made by phone on +971 4 248 9999.

What they offer

  • Personal Training: One-to-one sessions with the gym's personal trainers, who specialise in areas such as strength, HIIT, boxing and sports performance. Sessions start from AED320 each.
  • Group Strength Classes: Held at Move at FIVE JVC every Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The classes focus on building muscle and strength.
  • Gym Day Pass: Covers indoor gym access, one class, steam and sauna access and complimentary valet service for AED120.
  • Ice Bath: A 30-minute cold therapy session in the ice bath, offered for recovery at AED95.
  • HYROX Training Club: Weekly HYROX-style classes designed to build strength, endurance and race-day performance. Complimentary steam and sauna access is included after each session.

Good to know

  • Open daily 5 AM to 10 PM
  • Complimentary steam and sauna access after workouts
  • Free to join JVC Run Club on Tuesdays at 6 AM

What is the best gym in JVC?

The best gym depends on your goals, whether you want personal training, group classes, EMS sessions or race preparation. Read each entry in this guide and match it to what you want from your training.

Which gyms in JVC offer personal training?

Several of the gyms in this guide list personal training, including Prime Fitness, Circuit Fit, Move at FIVE and My30Minutes EMS Gym JVC. Check each provider's website for current trainer availability and session options.

Is there a gym in JVC with a day pass?

Move at FIVE lists a gym day pass, which is a useful way to try the space before committing to a membership. Confirm availability directly with the gym before you visit.

What is EMS training?

EMS training uses an electrical muscle stimulation suit while a personal trainer guides and monitors the workout. It may suit some people who want shorter sessions, but it is not right for everyone, so speak to a healthcare professional if you have concerns.
