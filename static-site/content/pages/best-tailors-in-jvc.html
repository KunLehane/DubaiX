Best Tailors in JVC

Alterations, custom clothing and everyday repairs in Jumeirah Village Circle

4 local options · Updated September 2026

A good fit can make a favourite garment feel new again. If you are looking for a tailor in JVC, this shortlist brings together four local options for clothing alterations, custom outfits and repairs, with addresses and direct contact details.

Start with the service you need: a custom suit, a more involved occasionwear fitting or a simple adjustment to everyday clothing. Follow each directory link for the business details.

How we chose these tailors

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked service test. We checked JVC locations and advertised services against official business or Circle Mall pages in September 2026. We have not tested their work in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and turnaround times with the shop.

Before you leave a garment

Ask for the total price, the collection date and whether a second fitting is included. Point out the exact adjustment you want and mention any delicate fabric, lining or embellishment. For a special event, leave enough time for a final fitting.

Where can I find a tailor in Circle Mall?

The Custom Shop is listed on the first floor. Alter & Spruce is on the ground floor, opposite Spinneys. Both appear in this guide, with separate directory profiles.

How much do alterations in JVC cost?

The work, fabric and construction determine the quote. Contact a shop with a photo or take the garment in; a simple hem and a lined jacket adjustment can require very different amounts of work.
