Dubai Martial Arts Guide

Best Martial Arts Schools in Dubai

Six dojos and clubs across Dubai, from kids' karate to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Tai Chi.

6 local options · Updated October 2026

Dubai has a martial arts scene to suit almost every goal, from children learning their first kicks to adults looking for a serious workout or a confidence boost. The six schools below teach everything from taekwondo and Kung Fu to Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, across Al Quoz, Jaddaf, Motor City, Al Barsha South and The Sustainable City.

The schools are listed in no particular order. We researched each one from its official website and have not visited or trained at any of them, so check current timetables, class availability and trial offers directly with the school before you go.

How we chose these

This is a researched editorial shortlist, not a ranked test, and the businesses are listed in no particular order. We checked locations, services and contact details against each business's official website in October 2026. We have not visited them in person. Confirm current opening hours, prices and availability directly with the business.

The Warrior Academy Al Quoz

A good fit for: Families and young children

The Warrior Academy Al Quoz teaches karate and taekwondo as part of a wider martial arts programme, using one syllabus for children and adults. Physical training builds strength and conditioning, and self-defence is taught in stages through the A.C.E system (Avoid, Control, Engage). Focus and self-control are practised in every session. Gradings are registered with the WKF and the UAE Karate Federation, so belts are recognised beyond the school. The academy suits children who want a dojo they enjoy attending each week, parents who want to train alongside their children, and students working towards competition karate.

Families can book a trial martial arts class through the website's Book a Class option. Classes are led by experienced instructors, and family classes put parents and children on the same mat, while adults are also welcome to train on their own. The school is located at Al Joud Centre, Office M16-1, 15A Street, Al Quoz, Dubai. Opening times vary by day, with weekend hours starting at 08:00.

What they offer

  • Kids karate and taekwondo classes: Children learn karate and taekwondo through one syllabus, with separate classes for Little Warriors (ages 2-3), Junior (ages 4-6), Senior (ages 7-9), Pre Teens (ages 10-12) and Teens (ages 13-18).
  • Family classes: Parents and children train together on the same mat, and adults are welcome to train on their own as well.
  • Self-defence training: Self-defence is taught in stages through the A.C.E system (Avoid, Control, Engage), so children learn to escape a grab and create distance.
  • Weapons classes: Weapons classes are offered for students aged 6 and over.
  • Sparring classes: Sparring classes are listed on the timetable for all ages.

Good to know

  • Classes capped at 13 students
  • Gradings registered with the WKF and the UAE Karate Federation
  • Open seven days a week
  • Monthly one-to-one parent calls

Midtown Martial Arts Club

A good fit for: Adult kickboxing and fitness training

Midtown Martial Arts Club teaches kickboxing and karate, with self-defence training also offered. Its motto is 'Fit for Life', and the classes aim to support a healthier and safer way of living, building fitness, strength and confidence for everyday life. The club states that its philosophy emphasises self-control, respect for others and a positive outlook. Students can train in kickboxing, karate and self-defence, with the aim of helping each person reach a level they are comfortable with.

The classes suit beginners through to black belts, and both adults and children are welcome. Kickboxing sessions run on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 7pm to 10pm, while karate classes take place on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from 4pm to 7pm, including an after-school kids group. Each class lasts one hour, and other times are available on request. To get started, prospective students can book a free trial or contact the team for more information. The dojo is in Jaddaf, Dubai.

What they offer

  • Kickboxing classes: Kickboxing classes are open to all levels and run for both kids and adults. Sessions are one hour long.
  • Karate classes: Karate classes are offered for all levels, including an after-school kids group. Adults can also join these sessions.
  • Self-defence training: Self-defence training is taught alongside kickboxing and karate to help build confidence for everyday life.
  • Personal classes: Personal classes are available in self-defence, karate and kickboxing for adults and children.

Good to know

  • Free trial session available
  • Monthly and annual memberships available
  • Closed on Sundays

Phoenix Black Belt Academy

A good fit for: Traditional taekwondo with belt progression

Phoenix Black Belt Academy says its single focus since it was founded in 2011 is developing students' physical fitness, confidence, good character and personal self-defence. Training uses traditional Tae Kwon Do techniques within a structured regime that aims to improve conditioning, stamina, balance and mental focus. Students progress at their own pace through the belt levels, and the academy describes the black belt as the end of a step-by-step path.

The academy runs three age groups for younger students: Mighty Tigers for ages 4 to 6, Children for ages 7 to 11, and Youth for ages 12 and over, who then transition to adult Tae Kwon Do. Adult students train for self-defence, weight management and stress management, and the academy highlights the friendships formed in class. To find out more, contact the academy by phone or email, or visit a class. Classes run on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday at Warehouse #14, Al Quoz Industrial Area 4.

What they offer

  • Mighty Tigers (ages 4-6): Fun and challenging martial arts classes for young children. The programme is designed to help children build confidence and make new friends.
  • Children (ages 7-11): Interactive martial arts classes that help children build confidence and make new friends. Students progress through the belt levels at their own pace.
  • Youth (ages 12+): A martial arts programme for students aged 12 and over, who transition to adult Tae Kwon Do as they progress.
  • Adult program: Classes for adults focused on self-defence, weight management, memory through learning new techniques at each belt level, and stress management within a strong community.

Good to know

  • Classes for ages 4 and up
  • Open Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday
  • Several membership programmes and special offers

CH Martial Arts Academy

A good fit for: Muay Thai and Jiu-Jitsu for teens

CH Martial Arts Academy teaches Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, a ground fighting and submission grappling art that the academy says emphasises technique and leverage over strength. Classes are split by age, from Little Grapplers for ages 4 to 6 to Youth Champions for ages 11 and over. Adults can join a male Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class for all levels or a Ladies Muay Thai class.

The academy is led by founder and head coach Hind Charifi, with coaches Ali Saryev, Aya Shanti and Hamza Tahir listed on the website. What martial arts are taught in Dubai?

Dubai's martial arts schools cover a wide range of disciplines, including karate, taekwondo, kickboxing, Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Kung Fu and Tai Chi. Many schools also offer self-defence and sparring alongside their core styles.

Are there martial arts classes for kids in Dubai?

Several schools in Dubai run programmes for children, with many grouping students by age so younger beginners train separately from older ones. Some also offer family classes, so parents and siblings can take part too.

Do martial arts schools in Dubai offer free trials?

Some schools offer free trial sessions, so it is worth asking before you sign up to a membership. Trial availability can change, so confirm with the school directly.

Is martial arts good for self-defence in Dubai?

Many of the schools in this guide include practical self-defence training alongside their main styles. Look for classes that suit your experience level and ask the instructors how they teach real-world techniques.

Can adults start martial arts classes in Dubai?

Adults can join beginner classes at most of the schools listed here, and several offer adult programmes or personal lessons. Choosing a class that matches your fitness and goals will help you settle in quickly.
