Best Indoor Golf And Simulators In Dubai

Golf
8 Picks

Dubai has indoor golf options for a night out with friends, focused practice and lessons. This list brings together simulator venues and coaching studios, with locations and links to help you choose.

Five Iron and MyGolf offer social golf experiences, while studios such as SwingFIT, Golf Kraft and Club Lab put more emphasis on practice, coaching or fitting. Check each venue’s website for current sessions, prices and booking availability.

Five Iron Golf Dubai

Five Iron combines simulator golf with food, drinks, lessons, leagues and group events. Its Westin Mina Seyahi venue has 17 TrackMan simulator bays, a sports bar, an outdoor terrace and mini golf. There is also a Business Bay venue at Damac Towers by Paramount. It is a useful choice when some of your group want to work on their golf and others are coming for the social side.

Aguila Performance

SwingFIT Academies

SwingFIT has indoor academies in Studio City and DIFC, with TrackMan bays, PGA coaching and swing analysis.
