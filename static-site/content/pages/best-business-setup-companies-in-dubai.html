How do I choose a business setup company in Dubai?

Start by listing the services you need, such as company formation, trade licence renewal or visa support, and check that each firm covers them. Then compare how easily you can reach the team, whether by phone, WhatsApp or in person.

What is a PRO service in Dubai?

A PRO service handles government-related paperwork for businesses and individuals, such as licences, visas and document clearance. Using one can save time when dealing with authorities that have their own processes and forms.

Can a PRO company help with a Golden Visa?

Several firms in this guide list Golden Visa support among their services, so it is worth checking the current requirements on official government sources. Ask any provider to explain their process clearly before you agree to anything.
