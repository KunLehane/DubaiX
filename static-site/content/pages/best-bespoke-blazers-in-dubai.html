Best Bespoke Blazers in Dubai

Six tailors making bespoke blazers and sport jackets across the emirate, researched from their official websites.

6 local options · Updated November 2026

From Al Quoz and Bur Dubai to DIFC and Business Bay, the options differ in location, service style and fabric choice. This guide brings six of them together in one place.

Suited and Booted Dubai

A good fit for: Bespoke suits and women's tailoring

Suited and Booted describes itself as a luxury bespoke tailor and ready-to-wear house with stores in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Its bespoke service is built around made-to-measure pieces that reflect each client's taste and style, with a focus on fit, quality and detail. The site lists casual wear, tuxedos, women's tailoring and wedding attire, and says the team of style consultants and master tailors will help clients curate their wardrobe.

The process starts with a consultation covering preferences, location, climate and occasion. Clients then attend three fitting sessions with the style consultants and master tailors before collecting the finished garment. Appointments can be scheduled through the website, WhatsApp, email or phone. The Dubai store is at Goshi Warehouse City, Warehouse 8, 19D Street, Al Quoz, and is open from 10 AM to 8 PM every day.

  • Wedding: Tailoring for wedding occasions, presented as a dedicated service on the website.

Stallion Bespoke

A good fit for: Multiple boutiques and mill-sourced cloth

Stallion Bespoke offers made-to-measure garments for entrepreneurs, executives, diplomats and other clients who want a garment designed around them rather than selected from standard sizing. Its services cover bespoke suits, shirts, jackets and trousers, along with wedding tailoring, corporate tailoring, VIP tailoring delivered to a home, office or hotel, and refinement services. Fabrics come from mills in England and Italy, including Thomas Mason and Ermenegildo Zegna.

A visit begins with a consultation covering your profession, lifestyle and what the garment is for. Appointments can be requested online for the Bur Dubai, Downtown Dubai, City Walk or Jumeirah Lake Towers boutiques, and the business states that it does not charge for consultations.

  • Refinement Services: Expert alterations and garment refinements executed with exceptional precision and care throughout.

Suits & Shirts

A good fit for: Blazers, sport jackets and summer linen

The business makes bespoke blazers and sport jackets alongside suits, shirts, trousers, tuxedos, linen suits and traditional wear. The service suits gentlemen who want clothing made to their own measurements, including those who prefer a visit to the tailor's own atelier or a fitting at home. Visitors can come to the Souk Al Kabeer shop, or request a traveling tailor who brings fabric to a home, office or hotel suite in DIFC, Downtown, Palm Jumeirah and Dubai Marina. The process includes body measurements, styling consultations and intermediate fittings. Appointments can be booked through the website or by phone or WhatsApp.

What is the difference between a bespoke and a made-to-measure blazer?

A bespoke blazer is drafted from a pattern created for one client, so it reflects their individual posture and proportions. A made-to-measure blazer starts from an existing pattern that is then adjusted to the client's measurements.

Can a bespoke tailor in Dubai come to my home or office?

Several of the tailors in this guide offer home, office or hotel appointments, including traveling fittings and concierge visits for members. Check each tailor's current options directly before booking.

Do Dubai tailors make bespoke blazers for women?

Suited and Booted offers women's tailoring, and House of Tailors makes made-to-measure and bespoke suits for both men and women. Confirm the range of women's jackets with the tailor you choose.

What should I bring to a bespoke blazer fitting?

Bring the shoes you plan to wear with the blazer and any shirt or accessories you will pair with it. This helps the tailor judge length, break and overall proportion more accurately.
