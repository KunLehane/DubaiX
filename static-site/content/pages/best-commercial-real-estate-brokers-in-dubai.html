How do I choose a commercial real estate broker in Dubai?

Start by checking whether the broker handles the type of space you need, such as commercial property for sale or rent. Then compare their services, office location and how clearly they explain their process.

Do commercial property brokers in Dubai offer property management?

Several of the brokers in this guide list property management among their services, which can cover looking after a space once it is let or bought. Confirm exactly what each service includes before you agree to anything.

Is a property valuation worth getting before buying commercial space?

A valuation can help you understand a property's worth before you commit to a purchase. Ask the broker how the valuation is prepared and what it covers.

Can a Dubai property broker help with mortgage advice?

Some agencies in this guide offer mortgage brokerage or advice alongside their property services. Ask who will advise you and how they are qualified to do so.
