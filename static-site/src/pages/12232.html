About Vast Manor Recording Studio

Vast Manor Recording Studio is a professional recording studio located in Dubai Sports City, offering vocal recording, music production, mixing, and mastering for artists across all genres - including Hip Hop, Trap, Arabic Pop, and R&B. Our studio is fully equipped with professional-grade gear and acoustic treatment, providing a comfortable, creative space for both emerging and established artists. We’ve worked with a range of local and international artists, and we’re trusted by clients across Dubai for high-quality, radio-ready sound.
