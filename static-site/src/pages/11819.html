Skillest Pro Am Golf Tournament Dubai - Golf Networking In March 2025 UAE

January 14, 2025 by Lehane

If you are looking for a golf networking event with a difference, this is the one for you.

The event is hosted over 6 days and you can play at some of the best courses on offer in Dubai.

Its a Pro Am event so each team will have 3 Pros and 1 Amateur.

The schedule for the event is as follows:

March 16th - Meet And Greet With The Pros And Amateurs at Icons Bar in Dubai

March 17th - Night Golf At The Faldo Course under the floodlights

March 18th - Dubai Creek And Yacht Club + A Private Brunch

March 19th - Majis Course At Emirates Golf Facility

March 20th - Final round at the Earth Course at Jumeirah followed by a BBQ and drinks + Prizes in the evening.

March 21st - Coaches Convention and Amateur Free Day to finish things off.

This is a great chance to get some networking in, play with 2 other Amateurs and 1 Pro.

Each team will have 3 Amateurs and 1 Pro. Only the top 2 scores will count in the Professional Individual Stableford event.

The cost of the event is £2499 per Amateur Twin Room.

For More Information, contact Andy@playgolfdubai.com
