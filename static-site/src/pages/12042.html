Best Pizza in JVC? Pizza Lord, Jumeirah Village Circle Reviewed

8.2
Jumeirah Village Circle - Restaurants & Hospitality - $

Pizza Lord is one of the best pizzas in JVC. Great value, elite toppings, a strong wider menu, and a proper dining room behind it. Easy to recommend, whether you get it delivered or eat in.

The story

Pizza Lord is German-born and moved to Dubai, setting up in JVC. It is one of those spots you hear about before you get there - when I first landed in Dubai they were running some great deals too, which made an easy first impression.

The pizza

This is the part that matters, and Pizza Lord delivers. The standout for me was the chicken and pineapple, which sounds like a strange combo on paper but absolutely works here. The base is filling without being too bready, the cheese and sauce are both spot on, and the toppings are honestly elite level. It is a proper, well-made pizza, not a thin disappointment or a stodgy one. Good taste the whole way through.

The value

This is where it really wins. The chicken and pineapple came with a can of Coke for around 32 dirhams delivered to the door. For a pizza of that quality, that is excellent value, and the prices across the menu are genuinely competitive. Quality and price do not usually line up this well, and here they do.

More than just pizza

The menu has proper range beyond the pizzas: burgers, pastas, salads, stuffed rolls, and fries and fry boxes. The dips are worth a mention too - I had the garlic dip with my delivery and it was excellent. So whether you are ordering in for one or feeding a group with mixed tastes, there is plenty to work with.

The restaurant

Dining in is a good experience in its own right. There is plenty of space, a nice atmosphere, and it is a comfortable spot to sit and eat rather than just a takeaway counter. So it works both ways - a solid delivery option on a quiet night or a proper sit-down feed.

The ratings

Location 7/10
Food 8/10
Atmosphere 7.5/10
Value 8.5/10
Overall 8.2/10

One of the best in JVC, and the value seals it.

Highlights

  • Elite-level toppings, especially the chicken and pineapple
  • Excellent value - around AED 32 for pizza and a Coke delivered
  • Strong wider menu: burgers, pastas, stuffed rolls, fry boxes, dips
  • Comfortable dining room, not just a takeaway counter
  • German-founded, now baking in JVC
