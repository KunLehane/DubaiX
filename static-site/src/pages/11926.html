Dubai BIGS Crypto Golf Networking Event This September In Dubai

August 19, 2025 by Lehane

This event is set to be one of the best golf networking events in Dubai in the month of September.

Dubai Bigs holds monthly events for the golf season in Dubai and this season will kick off with the Crypto Classic, which is sure to bring the best of the Crypto, Business and Golf Worlds together.

While the final details are yet to be finalized, we are super excited about the year ahead for both Dubai golf and Dubai Business Integrated Golf Society.

The remainder of the flagship events for the upcoming season are as follows:

September 2025 - Crypto Golf Networking Event, More News To Follow.
October 2025 - Gitex Investor Golf International
November 2025 - Aviation Open
December 2025 - Real Estate Masters
January 2026 - Logistics Masters
February 2026 - Healthcare Open
March 2026 - Media Masters
April 2026 - Capital Classic

To top all of these events off, there is also mouth watering prizes including Rolexes on offer at each event.
