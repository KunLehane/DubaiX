The Fore Golf Business Networking Review

Dubai Hills - Golf

FORE Business runs monthly golf networking rounds across Dubai, pairing a structured business introduction session with 18 holes. One of the best golf networking groups in Dubai if you want to grow a business while playing.

The Season Opener

We joined the FORE Business Golf UAE season opener at the fantastic Dubai Hills Golf Club, and it was an early start.

We met at the Hillside Cafe at 7am. When you attend, you get a chance to introduce yourself and your business. The group then discusses possible introductions and opportunities around the table that could help grow each other’s businesses.

Out On The Course

After the introductions we headed out to the range for a quick warm-up before setting off on the course in two three-balls.

It was a very close competition, but eventually one winner came out on top.

Where They Play

This was the first FORE Business UAE event back after the summer break. They also run networking rounds at Trump Dubai, The Els Club and Yas Links.

FORE Business began in the UK and has expanded internationally. In the UAE the groups meet monthly, and you can attend as a member or as a guest. There is a discounted rate if you sign up for multiple rounds across the month.

Membership

Membership is priced on how often you play. It is just over AED 1,200 for one round per month, or around AED 1,600 for two rounds per month. There is a discounted rate if you sign up for multiple rounds across the month.

Check the FORE Business site for current rates, as pricing changes.

The Verdict

This is definitely one of the best golf networking groups in Dubai. If you are looking to grow your business while playing some golf at the same time, FORE Business UAE is well worth a look.
