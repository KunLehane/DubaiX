Best Affordable Apartments to Rent in JVC

Real Estate & Property
9 Picks

JVC is where most people looking for value in Dubai end up, and studio asking rents across the community still start in the mid forties. These are the buildings offering the most for that money, cheapest first.

How We Chose These

Ordered by studio asking rent, lowest first. We have left out buildings that are cheap but that we assessed as poor value on viewing, since a low rent in a badly maintained building is not a saving.
